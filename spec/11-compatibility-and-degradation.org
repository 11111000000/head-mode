#+title: 11 Compatibility and Degradation

* Platforms and Emacs versions
- Emacs 29+ (treesit).
- GUI and TTY supported.
- TRAMP and daemon modes supported with graceful degradation.

* Degradation modes
- TTY: Text-only icon theme; echo-area tooltips.
- Missing fonts: Force text-only icons.
- Remote/TRAMP: Avoid heavy operations; simpler profile defaults.

* Coexistence and composition
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: composition
:END:
- head-mode coexists with other header-line producers.
- Provide a composition switcher (e.g., head-mode-toggle-composition) to select the active header-line source or to combine segments safely.
- When composing, ensure deterministic ordering and respect third-party faces.

* Provider presence
- If LSP not attached or delayed, treesit takes precedence automatically.
- If treesit grammar unavailable, use imenu/which-function fallbacks.

* Theming and faces
- Default faces inherit from standard header-line faces to integrate with popular themes.
- Users can override faces and icon themes.

* Accessibility
- Ensure all actions are reachable without mouse.
- Maintain legible contrast across dark and light themes (see [[file:07-icons-and-styles.org#faces][Faces]]).
